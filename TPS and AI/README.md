## Working method

Each claim follows the same structure:

1. **Original claim / hypothesis** — Terry's idea as first expressed.
2. **Research-based adjustment** — corrections, qualifications, and supporting
   distinctions discovered during discussion.
3. **Emerging implication for the talk** — a possible argument, still subject
   to further challenge.
4. **Questions still open** — items that require more thinking, evidence, or a
   deliberate choice.

A research-question document may remain outside this structure until Terry has
stated a hypothesis. It must be labeled clearly so an open question is not
mistaken for a claim.

When a claim is ready, change its status deliberately. Do not remove the
original claim; preserve the path from hypothesis to conclusion.

## Emerging direction — still provisional

Do not treat TPS as a recipe to transplant mechanically into software. Move
from concrete manufacturing mechanisms toward their operating principles,
management philosophy, and underlying reasoning. Then ask which ideas survive,
change, or become more important when AI makes candidate software solutions
cheap and fast to generate while judging correctness, usefulness, value, and
appropriateness remains comparatively expensive.
