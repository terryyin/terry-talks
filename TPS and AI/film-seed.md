#### Goal

Terry can share one accurate, compelling square film of 60–90 seconds.
After watching it once with English subtitles, a viewer understands the
diagnostic: teams should be more freed than constrained by what they build.
Jidoka offers a way to preserve discovered judgment in simple mechanisms,
stop known failures, and release developers for the next valuable problem.

  broad Freedom and Trust argument do not belong in this edition.
- Open concisely with the AI-right question and the freed-versus-constrained
  answer. Use a fully composed representative opening frame, rather than
