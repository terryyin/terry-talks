# Claim 12: Respect for People is respect for people who can think

**Status: Provisional — hypothesis stated; research not started**

## Original claim / hypothesis

> **TPS Respect for People is the realization that people who can think are
> the company's most important assets.**

The work itself demands and develops that thinking:

- JIT problem solving requires problem-solving skill.
- JIT collaboration requires facilitation skill.
- Kaizen needs analysis skill.
- Coaching needs coaching skill.

People who can do these things, and who keep acquiring more knowledge, are
the most important assets to the company.

This claim was split from [Claim 3](03-jidoka-enables-jit-trusts-respect-grows.md).
Claim 3's reconciled result is that jidoka frees people by empowering them,
JIT **entrusts** a resourceful response to real need, and Respect for People
helps them grow through challenge, teamwork, and kaizen. Toyota places mutual
trust under Respect. Respect alone does not guarantee growth. This claim asks
what Respect for People is *for*: not a soft accompaniment to jidoka and JIT,
but recognition that those pillars only work through people who can think.

## Emerging implication for the talk

If the claim holds, growth is not something “respect” causes by itself. It
is a consequence of giving people real problems, real collaboration, real
improvement work, and real coaching—and of treating the resulting capability
as the company's critical asset.
