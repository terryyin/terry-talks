# AI Augmented Development Pitfalls: the second film

## Purpose and source

Terry wants to continue the developer-facing series with a film based on
[Removing a Rule Is Not Adding Its Negation](../../AI%20Augmented%20Development%20Pitfalls/02-removing-a-rule-is-not-adding-its-negation.md).
Episode 1 established a realistic workplace storytelling format, a roughly
30-second portrait presentation, and distribution on LinkedIn, YouTube,
TikTok and Facebook. The second film should stand on its own for someone
encountering the series for the first time.

- **For / why:** Terry can share a short film that helps developers recognize
  how removing an obsolete rule can leave unnecessary tests and documentation
  behind when people or AI treat the removal as a new requirement about absence.
- **Outcome:** An editable film in Terry Moves, a reviewable preview, and a
  shareable portrait video export of approximately 30 seconds.
- **Storytelling:** Use a realistic development conversation and a visible AI
  assistant response. Show the old rule, the intended change, the mistaken
  preservation of the old concept through negation, and a clearer description
  of the current behavior. Choose the concrete scenario and dialogue during
  refinement, drawing on the source article.
- **Evaluation:** A developer watching without prior context can explain both
  the misunderstanding and its maintenance cost, and can name the corrective
  action: describe and verify the current capability, retiring assertions and
  explanations whose purpose has expired. Preserve useful regression coverage
  and meaningful negative requirements; avoid suggesting that every negative
  assertion is waste.
- **Presentation:** Portrait 9:16, suitable for phone viewing on the series'
  platforms, with readable captions, clear audible dialogue, and an AI-content
  disclosure where generated scenes or voices are used. Keep the conclusion
  consistent between narration and on-screen text.
- **Review:** Terry can review the preview before the edition is finalized.
  Carry forward Episode 1's lessons about a concrete opening, visible cause
  and effect, and mobile interface overlays.
