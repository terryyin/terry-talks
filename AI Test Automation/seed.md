**Goal:** Revise the finished Terry Moves film for large legacy systems whose
tickets arrive faster than the team can close them. Make Terry's conflict and
reasoning more engaging, the animation simpler, and ordinary intention-first
development clearly distinct from recovery of an overloaded legacy product.

#### Scope

Terry's review authorizes a new finished video, continuing the delegated
production, correction, commit and push authority from the original film.
Question first: "Ask AI to write more tests?" Then the hook: "You probably
don't want to do that." Acknowledge that missing automated tests may indeed
be a problem, before revealing the upkeep and complexity of generated code.
Do not imply that all early targeted tests are bad or all later tests useless.

Reliable automation requires high-level software engineering and protection of
the original intent. State the alternative before its prerequisites: AI performs
hands-on testing. Use an isolated, repeatable environment easy to set up; show
the AI checks people already know how to perform, then let it repeat them.
People investigate, confirm and fix findings, improving quality without adding
test code to maintain. Keep the article's qualification that environments and
checking still have costs, and intent can be recovered with human judgment.

After control returns, turn useful checks into repeatable test code that needs
no AI judgment to execute. For new features, tests express and protect the
developer's intent first and drive development. Keep optimizing and deleting
redundant tests; move suitable local checks to units while retaining distinct
end-to-end protection. End with spare AI used for simplification and fixes,
not indiscriminate generation: "Less to carry. Fewer bugs to chase."

Simpler visual focus, stronger contrast and causal acting; fewer simultaneous
props and technical labels. Replace stretching arms with fixed-length upper
arms/forearms, natural bends and reachable staging. Preserve recognizable warm
paper/ink art, readable captions, truthful Cedar credit and original restrained
sound. Aim near 70 seconds; measured speech may run to 80 seconds if clarity
requires it, with an 85-second production ceiling. No rushing or time stretching.
Terry's existing video remains available for comparison. No LinkedIn posting.

#### Key examples

- The viewer recognizes the tempting "more tests" diagnosis, agrees it can be
  valid, then sees new maintained code competing with the already growing queue.
- AI repeats a demonstrated check in a resettable isolated environment. A
  finding precedes human confirmation and a fix; no test-code pile is added.
- A learned check becomes ordinary executable test code; new feature intent
  becomes a test before feature code, then feedback and simplification continue.
- Across stop, pointing and working poses, limbs retain consistent segment
  lengths; actors approach props rather than stretching to reach them.
