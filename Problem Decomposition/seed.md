- **Goal:** Software developers and product people distinguish splitting a
  customer problem from structuring a solution, and explain why useful completed
  outcomes let feedback change direction affordably.
- **Scope:** One English square Remotion film, approximately 105–120 seconds,
  with a strong opening, mature original illustration, deliberate animation,
  legible captions, narration and restrained original sound. Reuse the dinner
  bill and customer-value continuity from Story Impact. A clearly identified
  synthetic narrator is acceptable for this delegated version; Terry has not
  supplied a new recording. Retain the full article and raw material.
- **Key examples:** Database, API and screen organize solution parts, while
  equal bill splitting solves a narrower external problem. Feedback moves
  payment tracking ahead of unequal shares. Stopping after equal splitting
  leaves that outcome useful and later scenarios unstarted. A vertical change
  touches the required parts. Multiple engineers finish one shared outcome.
  A commit serves a current purpose or an already documented pull, including
  product health; it strives for independent customer value. Healthy design
  retains speculative future options; unused speculative features can add
  complexity and close alternatives.
- **Important qualifications:** A plan for uncertain work is an attempt, not a
  guarantee. Seek recursively smaller user problems without assuming a solution.
  There are two goals. Safe stopping concerns completed useful boundaries and
  affordable redirection, not a universal zero-cost promise. One-piece flow here
  means finishing one customer outcome. The three Vs are Valuable, Visible and
  Vertical. Use story, scenario and slice; omit leaf. Option value is secondary
  and speculative, not a guaranteed saving.
- **Proof:** Focused timeline/content tests, existing product checks, an actual
  H.264 render and representative-frame inspection establish implementability,
  fidelity, readability and audio/video completeness. Review the full film
  against the article. Audience comprehension and Terry's artistic judgment
  remain evidence to gather after delivery.
- **Excluded:** Second language, external public release, remaking Story Impact,
  TPS/AI slide changes, and guaranteeing the world's best explanation. That
